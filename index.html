Dimensions & canvas size
×

    
Measure the surface roughly (a tape measure, or count tiles and boards) and enter width × height. The app gives the real aspect ratio and a canvas size to design your content at, so it fits the surface without stretching. Measure just one surface and Estimate the rest scales the others from where they sit in the projection (rough, because perspective skews it; ≈ marks an estimate). Template PNG saves a blank guide image at the canvas size with a real-world grid, ready to design on.

  


  

    Warp
    

    

      
      
    

    

    
Corner pin handles flat surfaces in perspective. Mesh mode adds a grid of points on top of that, so you can bend content around columns, curved walls or uneven shapes. Changing the grid size flattens the mesh.

  


  

    Edge blend & crop
    

    

    

    

    

    

    

    

    
The edge sliders fade the borders softly, which also works as feathering on a single projector. Split into 2-projector blend turns the selected surface into two overlapping halves that share one source, with matching blend ramps. With two projectors on an extended desktop, set Output to 3840×1080 so each half lands on its own projector.

  


  

    Masks
    

    

    

      

      

      

      

    

    
Masks apply to the whole output. Use cut-outs to keep light off windows, doors, TVs or people's faces. Drag the points, or use + Point to add one after the selected point for odd shapes.

  


  

    Reference photo
    

    
    

      

      

      

      
 Screen-blend in Edit

    

    
Take the photo from where the projector will sit. The photo shows only in Edit and Preview, never in the projector output.

  


  

    Preview / simulation
    

    

    

    

    

    
 Before / after

    
Preview simulates the result: the room is darkened, the projected light is tinted by the surface color in your photo, and it adds projector hotspot falloff, the faint gray glow a projector gives off even when showing black, and light bloom. With no photo it uses a plain gray wall.

  


  

    Audio reactive
    

    
    

    

    
Load a track, then turn up Audio on any surface so it pulses with the music. The Audio spectrum source draws live frequency bars.

  


  

    Cues / show
    

    
s

    

    
A cue saves the current look of every surface (source, colors, opacity, speed, audio, visibility). Press GO to crossfade to the next cue; click a cue to jump to it.
